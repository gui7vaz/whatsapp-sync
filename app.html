Following

Conversations are grouped by topic. A topic is also a destination: it has a folder, and a conversation can belong to several. Each topic suggests conversations whose name or recent messages mention its keywords; nothing is read or written until you click Follow. The arrow next to a name opens the conversation, the WhatsApp icon opens it in WhatsApp, and the pencil holds its history date, folders, Claude model and references.
